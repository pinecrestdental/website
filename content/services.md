# Dental care for every age in the house

We are a general practice. That means we handle the everyday work ourselves and send you to
a trusted specialist (we work closely with oral surgeons and orthodontists in Bend) only
when it's genuinely the better choice.

## Preventive care

- **Cleanings and exams.** Adult hygiene visits are 60 minutes; kids' visits are 45. A
  dentist examines every patient at every cleaning, not just once a year.
- **Digital X-rays.** Bitewings once a year for most adults, a full set every five years.
- **Sealants and fluoride varnish** for children, usually starting when the first permanent
  molars come in around age six.
- **Periodontal maintenance** every three or four months for patients with a history of
  gum disease, run by our lead hygienist, Maria Delgado.

## Restorative care

- **Tooth-colored fillings.** We no longer place silver (amalgam) fillings.
- **Crowns and bridges.** We scan instead of using goopy impression trays, and most crowns
  are ready in two weeks from a lab in Portland.
- **Implant restorations.** An oral surgeon places the implant; Dr. Okafor designs and
  places the crown on top, so your care stays with us.
- **Root canals** on front teeth and most premolars. Molars with curved or calcified canals
  go to an endodontist.
- **Dentures and partials**, including relines and repairs.

## Kids' dentistry

Dr. Lindqvist spent five years in a pediatric practice before joining us. We see children
from their first birthday, and the first "ride in the chair" visit is free. Parents are
welcome in the room for every visit.

## Comfort and cosmetic

- **Night guards** for clenching and grinding, made from a scan and fitted in one short visit.
- **Take-home whitening trays** with a professional-strength gel.
- **Nitrous oxide** for nervous patients of any age. For deeper sedation we refer to a local
  oral surgeon.

## Emergencies

Toothache, swelling, a broken tooth or a lost crown: call us at the start of the day and we
hold time every weekday for urgent visits. Existing patients can reach the dentist on call
after hours through our main number.

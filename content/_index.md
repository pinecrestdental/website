## A neighborhood practice since 2011

Pinecrest sits just off Neff Road, a few minutes from St. Charles and Pilot Butte, with
free parking right outside the door. Dr. Hannah Okafor opened the practice in 2011 and
Dr. Ben Lindqvist joined in 2018. Between them they see about 2,400 families from Bend,
Redmond, Sisters and Sunriver.

We keep our schedule realistic so nobody waits forty minutes with a magazine. We open at
7 a.m. four days a week for people who want to be done before work, and we stay open until
3 p.m. on Fridays. If you crack a tooth on a weekday, call before 10 a.m. and we'll almost
always see you the same day.

## Continuing education

Both dentists complete well over Oregon's required 40 hours of continuing education every
two years. Recent courses include digital crown design, pediatric behavior management and
sleep apnea screening. Our hygienists are certified to give local anesthetic and to place
sealants and fluoride varnish.

## Join us

We hire for kindness first. If you're a dental assistant or hygienist who wants a
four-day week in Central Oregon, send a short note and your résumé to
hello@pinecrestdentalcare.com.

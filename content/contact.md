## Getting here

We're on NE Wells Acres Road, two blocks north of Neff Road, in the single-story building
with the green awning. Parking is free in the lot out front, and there's a covered bike rack
by the door. Cascades East Transit route 3 stops at Neff and Purcell, about a five-minute
walk away.

The office is on one level with a ramp at the entrance and an accessible restroom.

## Canceling or rescheduling

Please give us 24 hours' notice so we can offer your time to someone on the waitlist. We
send a reminder text two days before every appointment; reply **C** to confirm.

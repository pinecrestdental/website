## Plans we're in network with

- Delta Dental of Oregon (PPO and Premier)
- Moda Health
- Regence BlueCross BlueShield of Oregon
- Cigna Dental PPO
- MetLife PDP
- Aetna Dental PPO
- Guardian

If your plan isn't on the list, we'll still bill it as an out-of-network provider, and most
PPO plans reimburse a large share of out-of-network care. Call Tessa with your member ID and
she'll check before you book.

We are not currently enrolled with the Oregon Health Plan (OHP). Our front desk keeps an
up-to-date list of Bend practices that are, and we're happy to share it.

## What a typical visit costs

| Service                         | Without insurance | With most PPO plans |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| New patient exam, X-rays, cleaning | $310            | $0 – $40            |
| Adult cleaning and exam         | $185              | $0                  |
| Tooth-colored filling (one surface) | $190 – $240   | 20% of the fee      |
| Crown                           | $1,350            | 50% of the fee      |

Estimates only; your exact cost depends on your plan's deductible and annual maximum.

## No insurance? Join the Pinecrest Plan

Our in-house membership covers two cleanings and exams a year, all routine X-rays, one
emergency exam, and 15% off any other treatment.

- **Adults:** $360 a year, or $32 a month
- **Children under 14:** $280 a year, or $25 a month

There's no waiting period, no annual maximum and no paperwork beyond a one-page sign-up.

## Payment options

We take cash, checks, all major credit cards, and HSA or FSA cards. For larger treatment
plans you can split the balance into three interest-free monthly payments, or apply for
CareCredit, which offers 6 to 24 months of promotional financing.
